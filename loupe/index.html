What we watch

The photos and artwork on the Etsy listings you paste in. We look for those same pictures on Temu, Amazon, SHEIN and similar marketplaces on a schedule.

02

What you get

The page we found, your original next to it, and the date we found it. If you mark it stolen, you can start a complaint when you are ready.

03

What we do not do

We do not file takedowns for you, and we never file one without you asking. We do not watch for similar ideas or lookalike designs, only your actual pictures.

Most weeks we find nothing. We tell you that too.
